Overview

About Family Fund

Family Fund gives the household a shared plan for big expenses such as education, weddings, and medical needs. Everyone can see the goals, the contributions, and the progress.

Who it is for: Households planning for children’s education, weddings, or a joint emergency reserve.

How it works

Four simple steps.

  1. Tell us your goalCall or message us with what you are saving for and by when.
  2. We size the planWe suggest an amount and time frame that fits your income.
  3. Review in writingYou get the plan, costs, and conditions on one page.
  4. Start and trackWe set it up with the provider and keep you updated on progress.

Availability, rates, charges, and eligibility depend on the partner institution and your profile. We explain all of them in writing before you commit.
