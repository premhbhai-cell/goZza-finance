Documents

Documents you may need.

  • ID proof (Aadhaar or PAN)
  • Address proof
  • Last 3 to 6 months of bank statements
  • Income proof: salary slips or ITR
  • Business proof and GST returns (business loans)
  • Property papers (home and property loans)

The exact list depends on the provider. We confirm it with you before you apply.
