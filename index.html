FAQ

Questions we hear most.

What is Gozza Finance?

Gozza Finance is a financial services company based in Faridabad. We help individuals, families, and small businesses with savings plans, investments, loans, insurance, and day-to-day money management.

Our approach is simple: explain every option in plain language, show the costs up front, and stay reachable by phone and WhatsApp.

What is Gozza Finance used for?

Planning and managing money: saving for goals, investing with guidance, borrowing with clear terms, protecting your family with insurance, and keeping a business’s books and payments in order.

What makes Gozza different?

We start with your goals, not a product. Every recommendation comes with the reason behind it, the cost, and the alternatives.

You reach the same team by call, WhatsApp, or in person in Faridabad.

How do I get started?

Open any service page, read how it works, and call us on +91 81301 12531 or message on WhatsApp. You can also use the enquiry form on the Contact page.

Which services do you offer?

Savings plans, mutual fund and stock guidance, gold savings, personal, business, home, and property loans, health, life, motor, and business insurance, bill payments, transfers, collections, accounting, tax planning, and payroll. See the full list on the Services page.

Do you charge for advice?

We explain every cost before you commit, including any fee we or the partner institution charge. Ask us on call or WhatsApp and we will tell you plainly.

Want a call back from our team?

Share your number and what you need. We will message you on WhatsApp to confirm a good time to talk.
